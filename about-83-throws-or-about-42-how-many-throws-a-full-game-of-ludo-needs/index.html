About 83 throws or about 42: how many throws a full game of Ludo needs

Ahmed Moaz · 16 November 2026

A game of Ludo ends when you bring your pieces home, and a piece needs a certain number of throws. I did the sums for a full set and for Quick Ludo.

One piece

With the exact-finish rule, one piece moving on every throw covers its 56 steps from start square to home in 20.76 throws on average. The median is 19. That figure already includes the wait for the exact throw at the end.

Four pieces

A full game gives each player four pieces. If each throw moves one piece, and none is captured or has to wait for a six, a player needs about four times 20.76, which is roughly 83 throws.

Two pieces

Quick Ludo gives each player two pieces. The same sum gives about 42 throws, half as many.

What the simple sum leaves out

  1. The wait for a six. Only a six frees a piece, and a six needs six throws on average. That adds time at the start.
  2. Captures. A captured piece returns to base and must wait for another six.
  3. Extra throws. A six, a capture or a piece home gives another throw, which speeds things up.
  4. Waiting at the end. While one piece waits for the exact number, another can move, which saves throws.

The result

The simple figures are a guide. They say a full game is about twice as long as Quick Ludo in throws, which matches the way it feels: about 21 minutes against about five.

The table log

Real games run 21.1 minutes at the median, with 2.4 real people at the table on average, in the table log for 13 September to 30 October 2026. With four people it would take longer.

What I take from it

If you want a game to be half as long, halve the pieces. If you want it to be a fifth of the length, play with fewer players or switch on extra throws.

The first two throws

The sum starts with the wait to leave base. With one try, 69.4% of players have no six after two throws, so the early part of the 83 throws is mostly waiting, and the 42 for Quick Ludo carries the same wait.

The sums in a line

Four pieces, 83 throws. Two pieces, 42.
