Nobody can copy your guess: how Draw and Guess hides a right answer

Ahmed Moaz · 10 November 2026

There is a quiet rule in Draw and Guess that keeps the game fair. When you guess right, the other players do not see your guess. They see that you have scored, and that is all.

Why it matters

In a chat-based drawing game, a right guess typed in the open is a gift to everyone else. They read the word and type it. The first player wins and the rest copy.

In Draw and Guess you type your guesses in the chat, but a right guess is hidden from the other players. It gives you points, and nobody else gets the word.

What you see if you are close

If your guess is one letter wrong, you see a message that says it is close. Only you see it. The others do not know that you are near, so they cannot piggyback on your near miss.

What this does to play

  1. Guess freely. A wrong guess costs nothing but time, and a right one is private.
  2. Use "close" as a clue. If you are one letter off, check the spelling or the plural.
  3. Do not watch the chat for the answer. It will not be there.

The drawer's side

The drawer cannot write the word in the drawing. Drawing letters is not allowed. That closes the other easy shortcut.

Scoring

A fast guess gives more points. You get fewer points if other players guessed before you. So a hidden guess does not stop the game from rewarding speed.

Why I care

Games that leak the answer teach players to wait. A game that keeps it quiet teaches them to guess. It sounds small. I think it is the difference between a game and a chat room.

What it means for the host

If you run a table of friends who compete, tell them the rule. A new player may type the word they think is right and wonder why nothing happens. The answer is that nothing is supposed to happen: a right guess is a private success, and the drawing carries on for everyone else.

What a guess is matched against

A guess is compared to the word loosely. Matching ignores case, accents, hyphens and apostrophes, so "cafe" matches "café", and nobody loses a point to a typing detail. What stays strict is the spelling of the letters themselves.

The rule in a sentence

Right answers stay secret, close ones whisper, and the word is never written.
