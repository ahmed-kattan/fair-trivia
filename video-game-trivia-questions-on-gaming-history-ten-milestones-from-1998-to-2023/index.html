Video game trivia questions on gaming history: ten milestones from 1998 to 2023

Ahmed Moaz · 3 November 2026

Gaming is old enough to have a history, and young enough that many of its landmarks happened in the last twenty-five years. This set of video game trivia questions picks milestones: a first, a surprise, a record and a deal. They run in date order, and each answer has a short note and a source page in the front matter. The whole trivia quiz takes about ten minutes to read out, which suits a pub quiz round or a classroom break.

The ten questions, oldest first

  1. Which 1998 Zelda game was the first in the series to be fully 3D?
  2. Sony began work on the PlayStation as a CD add-on for which company's console?
  3. Counter-Strike began in 1999 as a fan-made mod of which game?
  4. In 2005, a "virtual plague" in which online fantasy game was later studied by epidemiologists?
  5. Which console shipped with a bowling and tennis game in the box?
  6. Which farming game of 2016 was made almost entirely by one developer, Eric Barone?
  7. Which console launched in March 2017 alongside Breath of the Wild?
  8. Which 2022 FromSoftware game was named Game of the Year, with help on its world history from George R. R. Martin?
  9. Which 2013 Rockstar game made a billion dollars in its first three days?
  10. Which company bought Activision Blizzard in 2023 for about $69 billion?

The answers

  1. Ocarina of Time.
  2. Nintendo. The project was dropped, and Sony went on to make its own console.
  3. Half-Life. Counter-Strike started as a fan-made mod.
  4. World of Warcraft. The "Corrupted Blood" plague spread through the game in 2005, and researchers looked at how players behaved during it.
  5. The Wii, with Wii Sports.
  6. Stardew Valley. Barone worked on it alone for about four years before the 2016 release.
  7. The Nintendo Switch.
  8. Elden Ring.
  9. Grand Theft Auto V.
  10. Microsoft.

Which of these answers hold a lesson for the host?

Question 2 is my favourite. A company that was building a CD drive for a rival ended up making a console of its own and competing with that rival. That is a good moment to pause and let people react, because the fact reverses what most players assume about the two firms.

Question 4 is the oddest in the set. An accident in a game's code turned into a case study for how people react to a disease, since some players avoided cities and others tried to help. It shows that a trivia question about games can touch real science, which is why the topic works for a mixed family.

Question 6 carries a different point. One person, working alone for about four years, made a game that found a large audience. For a table with a young player who likes to build things, it is the answer to bring up afterwards.

How do the milestones fit together?

Three of them are about the way games are made. A fan-made mod became a famous shooter, a solo developer made a hit farming game, and a studio with a famous author helped write a world. Two are about money, and three are about the machines in the living room. If you want a theme, ask the table which kind of milestone they think matters most, then let them argue. Nobody is wrong, and the answers to the other questions will still be waiting.

What order works for a group?

Questions 1, 5 and 7 are easy for anyone who has held a Nintendo controller. Questions 3, 4 and 6 need a little reading about games. The last two are about money, and some players will guess from the size of the number alone. Put 8 last, since the name George R. R. Martin is a good surprise for anyone who thinks of him only as a novelist.

For a tie, ask how many years passed between Ocarina of Time in 1998 and Breath of the Wild in 2017. The closest guess wins. The answer is 19.

What I left out of the milestones

Ten entries from the video games file make up the list, each with a source link, and I went through them again on 27 October 2026. Deal values and first-week figures were reported at the time and are rounded. Sales rankings and the idea of a greatest game are not here, because those shift with the list and the date. A bit of general knowledge about games is plenty for the early questions.
