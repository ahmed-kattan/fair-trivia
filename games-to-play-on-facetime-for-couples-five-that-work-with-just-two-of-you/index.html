Games to play on FaceTime for couples: five that work with just two of you

Ahmed Moaz · 27 October 2026

Two people on a FaceTime call have a problem a group does not have: there is nowhere to hide. If a game is dull, both of you notice at once. So games to play on FaceTime as a couple need to be short, easy to start and good at making you talk. I build a browser game site on my own from Egypt, so I know what my own rules allow when only two people sit down. Here are five formats, and a few habits that stop a call from turning into a contest.

Why do two players need different games?

Many party games assume a crowd. A hidden-role game needs three people at the very least, and Who's the Spy online starts at three seats. A drawing game works for two, because one draws while the other guesses, and then you swap. A quiz works because you can race each other. A dice game works because it is a duel.

So the first filter is plain. Does the game's seat count start at two? If it does, it can be a game for a couple, even a long distance one.

Five games with a seat for just two

  1. A trivia race. Trivia Tavern seats 2 to 8 and lasts around 14 minutes. By default it is a race. You both answer, every right answer scores, and whoever is first scores most. A closest-number question closes every round, and the nearest guess takes it. The final round pays double, so a trailing partner can still catch up.
  2. A drawing duel. Draw and Guess takes up to twelve, but two is fine. The defaults are 100 seconds a drawing, a choice of five words, three letter hints and three rounds. You take turns at the pen. Count on 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. A short Ludo match. Ludo has 2 to 4 seats. The host can pick Quick Ludo, with two pieces each, which takes about five minutes. Turns run on a 30-second clock, so neither of you can sit and plan for ever.
  4. A luck duel. Snakes and Ladders takes 10 to 25 minutes. Nobody is better at it, which is the point. It leaves your attention free for the conversation.
  5. A slow property duel. Property Rush has 2 to 6 seats and a listed time near three-quarters of an hour. Nobody counts rounds, because the last player holding money wins. A smaller starting purse shortens it; the host can start everyone on as little as $500 or as much as $5,000.

Where do you find a table for two?

Boardit is where I keep all six games, and a guest only needs to type a name, so you can be playing within a minute of the call connecting. Keep FaceTime on your phone and open the game on a laptop or a second screen. The two jobs then never fight over one display. Nothing needs installing, so trying these online board games and free online games costs you nothing: no download, no account.

Without any screen at all

Some evenings you want nothing but the call. Three ideas suit a pair:

  • Twenty questions, where one of you thinks of a person or an object.
  • Would you rather, with questions you write beforehand.
  • A shared story, one sentence each, until somebody laughs too hard to carry on.

What keeps the score friendly?

Play a three-game match and stop. Let the loser pick the next game, so winning has a small cost. Or skip points on slow evenings and just count who made the other laugh. Couples tend to remember how a game felt more than who won it, so choose a rule that protects the mood.

Before you hang up

Pick the next game now, while you are still in the mood, and put a time on it. A call with a plan for next week is one both people turn up for. An evening for two only works if it happens twice, so fix the date.
