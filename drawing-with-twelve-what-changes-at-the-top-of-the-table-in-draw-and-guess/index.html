Drawing with twelve: what changes at the top of the table in Draw and Guess

Ahmed Moaz · 8 November 2026

Most tables for a drawing game have three or four people. Draw and Guess goes up to twelve, and the top end is a different game.

The arithmetic

Everyone draws once a round. With the default three rounds, twelve players make 36 drawings, which at the default 100 seconds is at most 60 minutes of drawing. That is 60 minutes of drawing at most, before choosing words and looking at results. The default table of eight plays 24 drawings, 40 minutes at most.

Real games are shorter. The median finished game in Boardit's table log, from 13 September to 30 October 2026, was 7.7 minutes, but those tables averaged 2.9 real people. A table of twelve is not in that median, so I can only give the ceiling.

What I would change for a big table

  1. Drop to two rounds. Twelve players and two rounds make 24 drawings, a comfortable length.
  2. Shorten the clock. Sixty seconds keeps the table awake.
  3. Add your own words. With twelve people, a shared joke goes a long way, and the list is hidden from the players.

Joining late

A friend can come in after the start. They guess right away and draw later in the round. For a big group where people drift in, this matters more than any other setting.

The scoring at scale

The first right guess is paid every speed point. The second is paid about 56%, the third about 38%, and the share never drops under 20%. So with twelve people, the early guessers score most and the later ones still score something.

Where to try it

A table this size is possible on Boardit's Draw and Guess, which takes up to twelve. It is the guess the drawing game for a big group. At the end, you can see every drawing from the game and save your favourites.

Ninety-six is too many

If a host picks the longest game, the arithmetic changes: twelve players over eight rounds make 96 drawings. Even at 60 seconds each, that is more than an evening, so for a table this size choose two or three rounds.

Before you start

Check that everyone can open the link. With twelve people, one bad connection is likely, and the game shows a green, yellow or red signal so you know.
