The Boardit Daily: five trivia questions, one a day, the same for everyone

Ahmed Moaz · 8 November 2026

If you like quizzes, a habit is easier than an evening. Everybody who opens the Daily on a given day gets the same five questions. It takes a couple of minutes.

How it works

You open the Daily and answer five questions. When you finish you see your score, and a share option leaves the answers out. Play on consecutive days and a run counter keeps count.

There is no table to join and nothing to set up. The Daily stays private to you.

A challenge for a friend

After the Daily, or after a game of Trivia on your own, you can challenge a friend. Your friend answers the identical questions and you can compare scores. It is a way to play together without being online at the same moment.

Why it works

Because the questions are the same for everyone, you can compare scores with anyone who played that day. It also gives a table something to talk about: which one tripped you up?

The questions are about well-known things and are not too hard, from nine categories including general knowledge, entertainment, music, sports and geography.

How I would use it

I like the Daily as a coffee-time habit. It takes less than the time to read the news. If you have a group chat, you can share your results there and see who scores best that day.

For a longer session, move to Boardit's Trivia Tavern, where up to eight friends can play at a table. A full multiplayer game, by comparison, had a ten-minute median from 13 September to 30 October 2026.

What it cannot do

It is five questions, so it is a snack and not a meal. If you want a long game, play a table.

What sits behind the five

Behind the five is the bank a table game uses. The question files hold 1,096 easy, 1,096 medium and 500 hard questions, and the Daily climbs from easy to hard, so the opening question is the gentlest.

A word on sharing

The share text shows your score and not the answers, so the people who have not played yet are not spoiled.
