A full round of Draw and Guess, scored by hand

Ahmed Moaz · 14 November 2026

It is easier to understand a scoring system by playing a round on paper. Here is one round of Draw and Guess with three players, a drawing time of 100 seconds and the default scoring.

The setup

Ana draws, and Ben and Cleo guess. A guess is worth 100 points plus 400 times the share of time left, rounded to the nearest five.

The guesses

Ben guesses right with 70 seconds left. His speed points are 100 plus 400 times 0.7, which is 380.

Cleo guesses right with 40 seconds left. Her speed points are 100 plus 400 times 0.4, which is 260.

What they score

Ben was first, so he keeps all 380.

Cleo was second, so she keeps about 56% of her 260, which is roughly 145.

What the drawer scores

Ana scores the average of the others' speed points: 380 and 260 make 320.

What it tells us

Ana scored more than Cleo. A drawing that is guessed quickly by someone pays the drawer well. Ben, who guessed first, scored the most. Cleo, who guessed 30 seconds later, scored far less. The gap between first and second is the big one.

What if nobody guessed

The drawer would score nothing. That is why the drawer wants a drawing that people can read.

What if Cleo had missed

The drawer's average would include a zero for her: 380 and 0 make 190. A missed guess costs the drawer half.

Where to play it

Boardit's Draw and Guess is free, for two to twelve players, with word packs and a recap gallery.

Try changing one number

Take the same round and change Cleo's guess to 55 seconds left. Her speed points become 320 and she keeps about 56% of them, roughly 180. Ana's average rises to 350. One extra quarter of a minute is worth 35 points to Cleo and 30 to Ana, which shows how closely the scoring follows the clock.

Hints and short words

Hints cannot give away a whole word. They are capped at half the letters, so a three-letter word gets one hint at most, and that is why a short word is a good choice for a quick round.

The round in a line

First guess pays most, and the drawer lives on everyone.
