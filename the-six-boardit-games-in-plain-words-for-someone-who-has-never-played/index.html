The six Boardit games in plain words, for someone who has never played

Ahmed Moaz · 17 November 2026

If you have never played any of these, the names say little. This is a plain description of each, in one paragraph, with the number of players and the usual length.

Ludo

A race round a track. Each player has four pieces. Ludo starts with a six to free a piece, and the first to bring all four pieces home wins. Pieces can capture one another. Two to four players, about 21 minutes in the middle.

Snakes and Ladders

A race up a board of 100 squares. You throw a die and move. Ladders lift you, snakes drop you. Nothing to decide. Two to eight players, about four minutes.

Property Rush

A trading game. You buy cities, collect rent from the others, build houses and try to be the last with money. Two to six players, about 30 minutes, longer with more people.

Trivia Tavern

A quiz. Each question offers four answers with one right, and speed pays. The last question of every round is a number. Two to eight players, about ten minutes.

Draw and Guess

One player draws a secret word, the others type guesses, and a fast guess scores more. Two to twelve players, about eight minutes.

Who's the Spy

One player, the spy, is told only the category while everyone else sees the secret word. You give short clues and then vote. Online it takes three to eight players and about seven minutes.

How to start

Open any of them with a link. A guest types a name. A guest needs no account. The times quoted are medians for finished games in the table log, 13 September to 30 October 2026.

Which to try first

If you are alone, try Trivia on your own or the Daily. If you have friends, Draw and Guess is the easiest for a first game. If you have an hour, Property Rush.

Where to start

You can play them all at Boardit, free in a browser.

The Ludo board in one line

Ludo is played on a 15 by 15 grid with a track of 52 squares, and that grid is the whole of what a new player has to take in. Everything else is dice and patience.

The six in a line

Race, race, trade, quiz, draw and bluff.
